Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

The short version

  • Bookbug HM is for Horace Mann accounts only. It uses your Google account to sign you in and save your books.
  • Horace Mann’s Bookbug HM administrators can read every book written in Bookbug HM. They can’t change anything, and every look is logged.
  • Your books in Bookbug HM are kept apart from any books in Bookbug on jake-goldwasser.com. Administrators never see those.
  • No ads, no tracking scripts, nothing sold, nothing used to train AI.

Who runs it

Bookbug HM (jakegoldwasser-hm.github.io/bookbug-hm) is a copy of Bookbug, a tool for writing and laying out books, for Horace Mann School. It is run by Jake Goldwasser, a teacher there, and it is in beta. This page explains what it keeps and who can see it.

Signing in

You sign in with your Horace Mann Google account (an @horacemann.org address); other accounts are turned away. Bookbug HM receives your name, email address and Google’s ID for your account, and uses them only to know whose books are whose, and to fill in the author on a new book. It never sees your password.

What Bookbug HM keeps

  • Your books, of every type (poetry collections, novels, essay collections, screenplays, plays, picture books, cartoon batches and comics): their titles, text, illustrations and drawings, layout and formatting, comments, saved drafts, and what you’ve archived or deleted in the last 7 days (30 for single entries), so you can restore it.
  • Your preferences, such as your tiers and the order of your shelves.
  • Read-only links you make to share a book. Anyone with such a link can read that one book until you end the link.

Your browser keeps a copy of your books, your sign-in and your preferences, so the page loads quickly. Signing out removes them from the browser.

Kept apart from Bookbug

Bookbug HM is stored separately from Bookbug at jake-goldwasser.com, even when you use the same Google account for both. Books you write in Bookbug don’t appear in Bookbug HM, and Horace Mann’s administrators can’t see them.

Horace Mann administrators

People in Horace Mann’s administration whom the school names as Bookbug HM administrators can read every book written in Bookbug HM, including archived and recently deleted ones, with their comments and pictures. They can only read: they can’t change, delete or share anything. Every book an administrator opens is recorded in an access log (who, what and when) that the administrators can see. Administrators must have an @horacemann.org account. Cassandra Parets is the primary administrator; she and Jake Goldwasser can name others, but Bookbug HM gives Jake no way to see anyone else’s books himself.

Google Drive and links

If you import from Google Drive, Bookbug HM asks for Google’s drive.file permission and you pick the files yourself in Google’s own file picker. It can read only the files you pick, can’t see anything else in your Drive, and never writes to it. If you import from a link, the page at that link is fetched through Bookbug HM’s server for you; only the text that comes back is kept, as part of your book.

What Bookbug HM doesn’t do

  • No ads and no tracking scripts. Nothing is sold, or shared with anyone other than the Horace Mann administrators described above and people you send a read-only link.
  • Nothing you write is used to train AI models.
  • Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where it’s stored

Everything Bookbug HM keeps is in a database on Amazon Web Services (AWS), in Amazon’s US East (Northern Virginia) region, in an AWS account belonging to Jake Goldwasser. Your browser talks to it only through one small program on AWS (an AWS Lambda function), always over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and Amazon keeps the database encrypted on its disks. That program also serves Bookbug, but keeps Bookbug HM’s books apart, as described above.

Because the database is in his AWS account, Jake could in principle open it directly through Amazon’s own tools. He doesn’t, and Bookbug HM itself shows him nothing but his own books. If the school would rather hold the data itself, it can be moved to an AWS account Horace Mann owns.

Besides AWS, Bookbug HM uses GitHub Pages, which serves the page itself (no writing is stored there), and Google, to sign you in, for the page’s fonts, and for the Drive file picker.

Deleting your information

Deleting a book in Bookbug HM sends it to Recently deleted, where it can be restored for 7 days before it is removed for good. To have all your information deleted, email jake_goldwasser@horacemann.org from your Horace Mann address; it will be removed within 30 days.

Students

Bookbug HM is meant for Horace Mann students and teachers. Questions about how a class uses it can go to that class’s teacher.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Questions: jake_goldwasser@horacemann.org.
